Study details & calculation

Developed in PDE5i-naive men with ED who completed 8 weeks of tadalafil 10 mg every other day and had an available outcome. Response means patient-perceived improvement on the Global Assessment Question (GAQ).

The study assessed 41 baseline variables. This calculator automatically derives 6 indices from raw measurements and supplies the 32 required predictors to the locked primary model. Extra lipid measurements are optional and do not enter this model.

Derived values follow the study precision: BMI, 1 decimal; NLR, LMR and PLR, 2 decimals; MHR, 3 decimals. Internal validation used outer stratified 5-fold cross-validation repeated 5 times, with inner 5-fold tuning.

Independent external validation is required before clinical deployment. The model has not been validated for other treatment regimens.
